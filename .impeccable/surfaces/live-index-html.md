## Direction contract

THESIS: The quiz is written live on the meeting-room whiteboard. Refuses the dark-stage-with-glow quiz app and the purple four-tile clone.

OWN-WORLD: Glossy whiteboard ground with faint ghosted erasures inside an aluminium frame, hung on a deep green office wall. Black marker for everything; one blue marker marks the active thing; green tick = right, red strike = wrong. Post-its (yellow, pink, blue, green, orange, lilac) are answer options, primary actions, toasts and the brand label. Round coloured magnets are players. Display in Permanent Marker, body in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. Raises: state is a mark not a hue (cutting bench); hierarchy by scale alone (specimen); one lit colour at a time (neon); board ground visible, no boxed cards (sampler).

STORY: People walk in, see the game code scrawled huge on the board, scan the post-it QR, watch their magnet appear, answer by tapping post-its, cheer at the tally.
